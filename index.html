About RETAINIQ

AI-powered personalized student retention planning designed specifically for distance-learning students — factoring in LMS engagement, daily routines, and working-student constraints.

The Project

RETAINIQ is an applied Data Science project developed to address a critical challenge in distance education: the silent, unmonitored dropouts of students due to isolation, heavy job shifts, and backlog accumulation.

Most educational systems only review results after semester finals, ignoring early engagement patterns like unviewed lectures, quiz skips, and assignment delays that lead to probation.

Our Solution

✓ AI generates 7-day academic recovery plans tailored to student constraints
✓ Filters routines based on working shifts, exam panic, and backlog levels
✓ Calculates baseline vulnerability using multi-variable logistic regression

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about RETAINIQ's student retention plans, academic signals, and privacy safety.

💡

General Inquiries

What is RETAINIQ? +

RETAINIQ is an early-warning student retention platform tailored for distance learners. It identifies quiet dropouts weeks before final examinations using 11 behavioral signals from LMS activity.

Is RETAINIQ free to use? +

Yes, it is 100% free for all students. There are no subscriptions, charges, or hidden paywalls.

Who is this platform designed for? +

It is built for students at distance universities (like Virtual University of Pakistan) who manage self-paced schedules and often juggle full-time or part-time jobs.

Does this replace my university LMS portal? +

No. RETAINIQ works alongside your university portal as an intelligent planning companion. It never requires your official university password.

📚

Retention Plans & Logic

How does the AI retention plan work? +

The system analyzes 11 signals (such as video watch percentage, submission delays, quiz averages, and job shifts) to compute a probability score and prioritized recovery checklist.

Can I generate a new recovery plan each week? +

Yes! We encourage re-checking your habits weekly. As you submit assignments on time, your risk score will visibly drop.

What happens if my risk score is above 50%? +

The AI Counselor will flag high-priority recovery steps: focusing on the top 3 highest-weightage assignments and shifting study sessions to early mornings.

👤

Academic Profile

What is semester baseline vulnerability? +

Historical data demonstrates that junior semesters (1st and 2nd) face significantly higher dropouts due to LMS adaptation issues than senior semesters.

How does part-time or full-time employment affect my metrics? +

Working more than 20 hours a week without scheduled micro-study sessions increases assignment submission delays by 42%. The model accounts for this by redistributing your study load into morning slots.

⏰

Study & Time Management

What is a realistic daily study budget? +

For distance learners, 2.5 to 3 focused daily hours broken into morning and evening Pomodoro intervals is more than sufficient for a 3.2+ CGPA.

How does the 5-minute activation rule work? +

Whenever you feel stuck, commit to reading just one slide for 5 minutes. Action triggers psychological momentum, eliminating study paralysis.

🔒

Account & Privacy

Is my academic data stored on a server? +

No. All machine learning inference runs client-side directly inside your browser. No sensitive academic marks are transmitted to remote servers.

Can I delete my stored simulation history? +

Yes. You can clear all locally saved logs with a single click in the Settings tab via "Reset Simulation History".
