About RetainIQ

Empowering distance learners through transparent educational machine learning.

The Problem

In distance learning environments like the Virtual University of Pakistan, students often drop out quietly. Without physical classrooms, signs like missed lecture videos, delayed assignment submissions, and isolated study habits go unnoticed until final exams.

Our Solution

RetainIQ provides an early-warning retention system that flags dropout risk early in the semester. Instead of giving up, students receive tailored recovery checklists, micro-study plans, and empathetic mindset reframing to graduate safely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the RetainIQ retention algorithm.

Is my academic data safe and private?

Yes, 100%. The machine learning weights run locally inside your browser using lightweight JavaScript math. Your grades, LMS logins, and personal notes are never uploaded to any remote server.

How is the dropout risk score calculated?

The model evaluates 11 distinct signals—including assignment submission delays, LMS login regularity, video watch percentage, quiz averages, and job hours—based on trained logistic regression coefficients from distance learners.

Can this help working students manage time?

Absolutely. The built-in Study Optimizer tool builds customized 25-minute Pomodoro modules and Fajr/after-work routines specifically for students working full-time or part-time jobs.
