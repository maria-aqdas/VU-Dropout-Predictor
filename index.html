    Data & model

    Trained on a synthetic dataset of 3,000 student-terms, built to mirror correlations reported in distance-education dropout research (LMS engagement, submission timeliness, GPA trajectory). Real, anonymized university engagement logs would replace this in a production version — this stands in until that data-sharing agreement exists.

    Why client-side

    The trained logistic regression's weights are exported as plain numbers and the inference runs directly in your browser — no server, no API cost, no cold starts. That's what makes this deployable for free, and it's fast enough to update the score on every slider move.
