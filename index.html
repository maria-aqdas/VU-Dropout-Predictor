Why students drop out

📉GPA trend drop is the strongest predictor (19%). A sudden fall signals burnout.
📝Missing assignments (15%) is the clearest proof of lost study discipline.
🎬Low video watch % (15%) means the student is no longer following weekly material.

Advisor checklist

1️⃣Flag anyone above 60% risk before week 6.
2️⃣Use What-If to show the student a concrete path back.
